# The Career Waze Initiative: A Google & ADP Partnership

## Our Mission

Our objective is to support the upcoming workforce by providing clarity on potential career trajectories. By combining ADP's unmatched workforce insights with Google's cutting-edge AI technology, we are committed to helping every individual realize their full potential through comprehensive career mapping.

## The Vision: Addressing Workforce Anxiety

The workforce faces growing anxiety about the future of work due to rapid technological shifts and AI. We envision AI as a "guide" empowering everyone — from students to professionals — to navigate careers with clarity. Our goal is to unlock individual potential and address systemic inequalities through personalized career design.

## Initial Focus: The Early-Career Journey

Initial focus is on early-career individuals (students and recent graduates), prioritizing the challenge of finding and securing internships and first jobs. This addresses a significant societal need, builds a foundational dataset, and establishes a joint brand to curate future talent.

## Key Personas

- **Individuals:** Students, job seekers, and professionals seeking career growth.
- **Employers:** A special focus on SMBs seeking to build their future talent pipeline.
- **Mentors:** Experienced professionals willing to guide and support the next generation.

## Our Approach: Building the "Waze for Careers"

"Career Waze", a revolutionary platform, applies data-driven mapping to professional growth.

- **Unprecedented Data:** Utilizes ADP's anonymized data from 25M+ employees to map and identify previously invisible historical career trajectories and open roles.
- **Google AI Power:** Leverages Gemini models and infrastructure for personalized guidance, skill gap identification, and resource recommendations.
- **Massive Distribution:** Combines Google's consumer reach (Search, Workspace) with ADP's access to one million businesses.

## Core Principles

1. **Individual First:** Prioritize user empowerment with career prep and negotiation tools.
2. **Democratize Opportunity:** Connect talent with roles, especially at SMBs who are challenged with identifying potential candidates.
3. **Equity & Fairness:** Use data to mitigate bias and address pay inequities.
4. **Mentorship Ecosystem:** Connect aspiring talent with experienced professionals to foster a cycle of mentorship.

## Value Proposition

| For the Student | For the Company |
|---|---|
| **Find Your Path:** Discover personalized internship and first-job opportunities, including hard-to-find roles at small and medium-sized businesses. | **Secure Top Talent:** Hire the desired candidate, leading to shorter, more efficient hiring cycles. |
| **Gain a Competitive Edge:** Bridge skill gaps with custom recommendations and use AI tools for interview preparation. | **Improve Recruiter & HM Success:** Empower recruiters and hiring managers to effectively close candidates. |
| **Connect with Mentors:** Receive expert guidance to navigate your early career with confidence. | **Increase Retention:** Start the employee relationship on a positive, mutually-respectful note, leading to better long-term retention. |
| **Negotiate Like a Pro:** Master offer evaluation and practice safe negotiation to ensure fair, equitable compensation. | **Guidance:** Identify possible gaps in hiring strategy by understanding available talent pool. |
| **Explore Your Future:** Explore potential career paths based on millions of real-world journeys to make informed future decisions. | **Flag High Intent:** Identify students who show highest interest in your company and open role/s. |

## Open Data Source Questions for ADP

- **Career Trajectories:** What career path data can we create to show the first move for a new grad as well as longer-horizon trajectory? Consider MyCareerConnect but it needs to generalize across industries and company sizes.
- **Salary Benchmarks & Negotiation:** What compensation data can we surface to job seekers? Can we also see how often early-career candidates negotiate and the outcomes?
- **SMB Job Listings & Organizational Gaps:** What signals could help predict when an SMB is likely to hire before a listing exists? Can we also flag structural gaps — e.g., a role that competitors of similar size commonly have but they don't?
- **Mentor Identification:** What data signals could help identify strong mentor candidates who haven't self-identified as one?
- **Workforce Trends & Skill Gaps:** What trend data do we have on emerging roles, in-demand skills, and hiring patterns that could feed personalized recommendations to students and recent grads?
